✅ Automated Process
📧 Invoice emails received from branches (CRM / email)
↓
⚙️ Google Apps Script reads & parses invoice data automatically
↓
📋 Script maps fields → FK / OF / LT rows in correct e-Faktur CSV format
↓
🔍 Auto-validation: DPP, PPN calculation, NPWP & masa pajak checked
↓
✅ CSV ready for bulk import to DJP e-Faktur in one click
Benefits: Near-zero manual entry · Handles all branches in one batch · Consistent format · Faster cycle close
